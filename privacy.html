Who this is about

The tool has a single user: the owner of the Twice Told YouTube channels, who runs it on his own computer. It does not collect, receive or process data about anyone else.

Google account data the tool accesses

When the owner connects one of his channels, Google asks him to grant the tool access to manage his YouTube account. With that permission the tool:

  • uploads videos to his channels and sets their title, description, tags, language, thumbnail and scheduled publish time;
  • reads the status of those videos (for example whether they were processed, restricted or removed);
  • reads basic information about his channels (name, identifier, status).

It does not read comments, messages, viewers' data, watch history or any data of other users.

How the data is used

Only to publish and monitor the owner's own videos, as described above. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or to train machine-learning models.

How the data is stored

The authorisation tokens issued by Google are stored in a file on the owner's computer, readable only by his user account. Nothing is stored on a server and nothing is kept on this website.

Sharing

The data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account settings, under Third-party apps and services. Deleting the token file on the computer removes the stored authorisation.

This website

These pages are static: they set no cookies and run no analytics.
