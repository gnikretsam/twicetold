Twice Told publishing tool

A private tool that publishes videos to the Twice Told YouTube channels.

Privacy policyTerms of service

What it is

Twice Told is a group of YouTube channels that publish narrated stories in several languages (Twice Told Italia, Twice Told English). This tool runs on the channel owner's own computer and uploads the videos he produces to those channels.

What it does with YouTube

The tool uses the YouTube Data API, with the channel owner's permission, to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channels;
  • set each video's title, description, tags, language, thumbnail and scheduled publish time;
  • read the status of the videos it uploaded and of the owner's channels.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public and has no sign-up: nobody else is asked to connect a Google account.

Privacy
